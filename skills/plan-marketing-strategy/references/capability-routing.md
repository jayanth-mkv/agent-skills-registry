# Marketing Capability Routing

Use this reference to locate the smallest set of guidance needed for a marketing task. Select one primary capability, add only the supporting capabilities required by dependencies, and keep the result tied to one business or customer outcome.

## 1. Routing method

Route in this order:

1. Name the decision or deliverable.
2. Identify the customer-journey stage and binding constraint.
3. Choose one primary capability from the map below.
4. Add a supporting capability only when the work crosses a real dependency.
5. Load the corresponding detailed references from the main skill.
6. Verify current facts, policies, and technical requirements before execution.

Do not turn every request into a full marketing transformation. A landing-page rewrite may need positioning, page, and conversion guidance; it does not automatically need a new brand, channel plan, or analytics stack.

## 7. Market and business-model adaptation

Adapt any capability for:

- self-serve, subscription, usage-based, sales-led, and hybrid software;
- ecommerce, direct-to-customer, retail, wholesale, and repeat-purchase products;
- mobile apps, games, and multi-device products;
- marketplaces and other multi-sided networks;
- local, multi-location, franchise, and service-area businesses;
- professional, managed, and project-based services;
- enterprise and account-based buying;
- developer products and open ecosystems;
- media, education, membership, and community businesses;
- nonprofit, public-interest, and behavior-change programs;
- new categories, mature categories, regulated markets, and international expansion.

Use the market, model, and launch reference to change the journey, proof, economics, channels, team, and measurement—not merely the vocabulary.

## 8. Coverage and exclusion rules

- Treat platform names, feature availability, specifications, benchmarks, policies, and legal requirements as live facts to verify.
- Treat templates as starting structures. Adapt them to audience, evidence, business model, and constraints.
- Do not recommend every listed capability. Choose the smallest coherent set that addresses the constraint.
- Do not create fake reviews, links, engagement, accounts, demand, urgency, scarcity, testimonials, experts, or customer evidence.
- Do not use mass-generated pages, messages, or creative when each unit lacks useful differentiation and quality control.
- Do not disguise advertising, automate high-impact actions without authority, or bypass consent and platform enforcement.
- Do not draft legal policy language as if it were approved legal advice. Build an issue checklist and route it for qualified review.
- Do not require a permanent context document, agent loop, simulated personality panel, software runtime, or external integration.
