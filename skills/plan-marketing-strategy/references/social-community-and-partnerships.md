Use this reference to build trust-based distribution through participation, creators, customers, partners, earned media, and live experiences.

## 1. Choose the relationship motion

| Motion | Value exchanged | Best fit | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Organic social | Useful ideas, access, entertainment, or conversation | Audience already pays attention on a platform | Broadcast calendar with no native value |
| Community | Peer help, identity, contribution, recognition | Members benefit from one another | Audience-shaped support or promotion channel |
| Employee/customer contribution | Lived experience and credible perspective | Contributors have real knowledge and support | Forced advocacy or undisclosed incentives |
| Creator/influencer | Creative interpretation and trusted access | Audience and product fit are strong | Buying reach without fit, rights, or disclosure |
| Ambassador | Ongoing relationship and participation | Authentic long-term alignment exists | Treating advocates as low-cost media inventory |
| Referral | Customer-to-customer recommendation | Delivered value is worth sharing | Incentive attracts abuse or poor-fit customers |
| Affiliate | Performance-based promotion | Economics and tracking support partner selling | Misleading claims, brand bidding, or last-click conflict |
| Co-marketing | Complementary audience and joint value | Partners share a real customer problem | Logo swap without a useful shared offer |
| Channel or integration partner | Distribution plus implementation value | Combined solution improves the outcome | Unclear ownership and broken handoffs |
| Education program | Skills, certification, curriculum, or access | Learning increases adoption or ecosystem quality | Promotional content presented as neutral education |
| Earned media | Timely evidence, expertise, or public relevance | There is a real story or useful source | Treating distribution as newsworthiness |
| Event or webinar | Live learning, demonstration, or connection | Presence improves understanding or trust | Optimizing registrations without attendance or follow-up |

Choose one primary motion based on audience relationship, customer value, trust transfer, operational capacity, and measurable next behavior.

## 2. Plan organic social

Define:

- audience and native reason they use the channel;
- account role: brand, leader, expert, employee, product, or community;
- three or fewer content jobs;
- repeatable series and source material;
- native formats and accessibility requirements;
- conversation and response policy;
- destination and capture only when useful;
- production, review, publishing, and moderation owners;
- learning metrics and cadence.

Use content jobs such as:

- teach a method;
- show the work or product;
- interpret a relevant change;
- provide original evidence;
- tell a customer or team story;
- answer objections;
- invite contribution;
- build anticipation for a legitimate event or release.

Open with clear relevance, deliver value in the native post, and make links optional when the task can be completed without leaving. Adapt one source artifact to each format; do not mechanically duplicate copy across channels.

Use social listening to capture questions, language, complaints, competitor changes, category narratives, and emerging needs. Define the sample and limitations; loud conversations are not automatically representative.

Do not manufacture engagement, impersonate customers, hijack unrelated trends, or automate replies that pretend to be personal.

## 3. Build community value

Write a community contract:

```markdown
## Member and organizational outcome
## Who belongs and who does not
## Member-to-member value
## Core behaviors, rituals, and resources
## Host role and service level
## Rules, moderation, privacy, and escalation
## Onboarding and first contribution
## Recognition and leadership paths
## Product or commercial boundary
## Health, harm, and outcome metrics
```

Start with a recurring valuable interaction before selecting software. Seed with members who share a real problem and can help one another. Design welcoming, discovery, contribution, feedback, recognition, conflict, and exit experiences.

Track active contributors, helpful responses, time to first value, repeat participation, member outcomes, safety incidents, support burden, and qualified product impact. Membership count alone is not community health.

For participation in an existing community, disclose affiliation, follow rules, answer the actual question, and avoid coordinated promotion.

## 4. Run creator and contributor programs

Distinguish:

- **customer contribution:** experience from a real customer;
- **employee contribution:** expertise or behind-the-scenes perspective;
- **creator partnership:** creative production and audience access;
- **influencer activation:** paid or gifted endorsement and distribution;
- **ambassador program:** repeated relationship and community participation.

Select contributors using:

- audience overlap and trust;
- content and subject fit;
- genuine product experience;
- quality and consistency;
- reputation and brand-safety review;
- audience authenticity and geographic fit;
- working style, rights, cost, and operational reliability.

Create a brief that preserves creator judgment:

```markdown
Outcome and audience
Required truthful product facts
Desired customer response
Offer, destination, and tracking
Mandatory disclosure and placement
Claims and prohibited representations
Deliverables, dates, revisions, and approvals
Usage, editing, paid amplification, territory, and duration rights
Safety, comments, escalation, cancellation, and payment
Measurement and post-campaign learning
```

Do not script a personal opinion the contributor does not hold. Do not alter a testimonial so its meaning or typicality becomes misleading.

## 5. Design referral, affiliate, and partner programs

### Referral

Define the eligible advocate, referred person, qualifying event, reward timing, mutual value, limits, fraud rules, support, and tax or disclosure review. Reward a customer-quality event rather than a low-friction action when abuse risk is high.

### Affiliate

Define:

- partner profile and prohibited partner types;
- commission basis, attribution, reversals, and payment;
- permitted claims, content, bidding, domains, coupons, and placements;
- disclosure and brand use;
- tracking, duplicate attribution, fraud, and audit;
- activation, education, support, and termination.

### Co-marketing and channel partnerships

Assess audience overlap, complementary value, reputation, strategic fit, operational capacity, and conflict. Build a joint value proposition, journey, asset plan, lead/account rules, ownership, service levels, budget, approvals, and post-program reuse.

When participant or lead data crosses organizations, define a handoff contract:

```markdown
## Shared purpose and prohibited uses
## Data fields, collection notice, permission, and source of truth
## Transfer method, encryption, access roles, and audit
## Identity, duplicate, customer, partner, and suppression handling
## Routing, acceptance, rejection, response, and escalation service levels
## Preference, objection, correction, retention, deletion, and proof of completion
## Incident detection, containment, notification, and responsible owners
## Reconciliation, reporting, end date, and data return or destruction
```

### Integration and education ecosystems

Make the combined workflow or learning outcome the product. Define technical readiness, documentation, support, listing accuracy, curriculum quality, instructor or partner qualification, completion, certification integrity, and adoption path.

Do not launch a partner program before the internal owner can recruit, onboard, support, reconcile, and enforce it.

## 6. Earn media and public attention

Choose a real angle:

- material product, company, research, customer, or market news;
- original data or analysis;
- timely expert interpretation;
- access to a credible person or demonstration;
- public-interest resource;
- local or sector relevance;
- a response to a developing story when the organization has legitimate expertise.

Build:

- one-sentence reason the audience should care now;
- verified facts, evidence, dates, and limitations;
- spokesperson, availability, and approved quotes;
- audience-specific pitch and supporting assets;
- press page or media kit with current information;
- response, correction, and follow-up owner.

Research each outlet or journalist's actual audience and recent work. Offer a relevant angle without fake familiarity, mass personalization, pressure, or hidden payment.

Do not exploit tragedy, manufacture controversy, issue empty announcements, fabricate exclusivity, or imply editorial coverage is independent when it is paid.

## 7. Plan events and recurring experiences

Define:

```text
target participant -> reason to attend -> live value
-> participation -> next useful action -> follow-up -> retained value
```

Plan:

- format, topic, host, contributors, and accessibility;
- registration data and consent;
- audience acquisition and partner roles;
- attendance reminders and calendar handling;
- run of show, moderation, captions, interpretation, recording, and contingency;
- participant questions, networking, demonstration, or workshop outputs;
- follow-up branches for attended, registered-not-attended, contributed, and sales-ready states;
- content reuse and rights;
- attendance, engagement, satisfaction, qualified action, and downstream value.

For podcasts and recurring shows, add editorial premise, guest criteria, production cadence, distribution, discoverability, clips, transcript, and archive ownership.

## 8. Measure relationship quality

Use a journey view:

```text
eligible audience -> reached -> meaningfully engaged
-> participated or referred -> qualified action
-> experienced value -> retained or advocated
```

Measure by motion:

- social: relevant reach, qualified engagement, profile or destination quality, assisted outcomes;
- community: member outcomes, contribution, response quality, retention, safety;
- creator: content quality, audience fit, incremental reach, qualified action, rights value;
- partner: recruited, activated, productive, pipeline or customer quality, partner retention;
- referral/affiliate: advocate activation, referred quality, fraud, margin, incremental contribution;
- earned media: audience relevance, message accuracy, referral and branded demand, relationship value;
- events: attendance, participation, next actions, opportunity or product outcomes.

Separate organic, paid amplification, partner, affiliate, and referral attribution. Use holdouts or geographic/time comparisons where feasible. Do not infer causality from tracked links alone.

## 9. Govern disclosure, rights, and abuse

Before launch verify:

- material relationships are clear, prominent, understandable, and present in the same language and medium;
- reviews, testimonials, ratings, and experiences are genuine and not selectively altered;
- claims are supported and within each contributor's experience;
- content, music, likeness, logo, product, editing, reuse, and amplification rights are documented;
- participant and lead data has a valid purpose, access control, retention, preference, and deletion path;
- incentives, contests, rewards, discounts, and commissions receive current jurisdiction and platform review;
- partner, creator, employee, affiliate, and community conduct can be monitored and corrected;
- brand safety, harassment, misinformation, conflict, fraud, and crisis escalation have owners;
- public corrections and takedowns can be made quickly.

Do not assume a platform disclosure control is sufficient. Do not suppress honest negative reviews, condition support on review changes, or reward only positive sentiment.
