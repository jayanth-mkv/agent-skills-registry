# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Agent Skills Registry a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include empathy, respectful feedback, clear technical disagreement, acceptance of responsibility, and attention to what benefits the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, personal or political attacks, trolling, insulting comments, publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s GitHub Security Advisory channel. Maintainers will respect the reporter’s privacy and respond in a fair, timely manner.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant version 2.1.
